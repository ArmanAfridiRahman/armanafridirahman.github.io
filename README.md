## What the site shows

A single page with a 3D chip you can turn in your hands, and working demos that show how the
systems behind the case studies behave:

- **Bulk email, SMS and WhatsApp from a weak server**: blast it, or pace it?
- **Per-employee time and payslips**: from hours to payslip, with a real PDF.
- **Restaurant SaaS with a QR code per table**: sort, scan, order.
- **Ecommerce**: payment gateways with failover, and warehouse picking.
- **WordPress plugins and themes.**
- **Interface decisions**: the same form, twice.
- **Sub-agents, plugins and patterns for AI.**
